# Multichannel SDR Orchestration Growth Skill

## Origin & Context
Adapted from enso.bot research record **EXP-003** (*EMAIL · LINKEDIN · SMS · VOICE / The SDR no human can run: 27 booked meetings*).

## Objective
Orchestrate high-precision, low-volume, signal-driven outbound engagement across channels (Email + LinkedIn) targeting active buyers and technical decision-makers (CTOs, Head of AI, Lead Security Architects) who have exhibited immediate buying signals.

## Methodology & Execution Flow

### 1. Real-Time Signal Ingestion
- Ingest signal events: new job postings mentioning "AI Agent Security" or "MCP Server", repository creations with agentic frameworks, or funding announcements.
- Enrich company profile and map verified technical decision-makers.

### 2. Multi-Touch Sequence Design
- **Touch 1 (LinkedIn Connection):** Contextual invitation referencing a specific technical challenge or open-source RFC.
- **Touch 2 (LinkedIn Interaction):** Thoughtful comment on the prospect's recent technical post.
- **Touch 3 (Direct Outreach):** Focused, 3-sentence email addressing their immediate security stack gap, referencing our open-source broker solution.

### 3. Strict Signal vs. Spam Ratio
- No bulk spamming or generic email blasts. Target limit: 5–10 highly researched accounts per week.

## Rules & Constraints
- **Absolute Anti-Spam Guard:** Outreach must be personalized, technically substantive, and strictly relevant.
- **Approval Gate:** Outbound sequences, message copy, and prospect lists require Ziv's explicit review and approval before sending.
