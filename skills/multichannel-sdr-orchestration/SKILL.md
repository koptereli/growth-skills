# Multichannel SDR Orchestration & Signal Intelligence

## Overview
Autonomous outbound workflows deliver superior conversion when driven by real-time buying signals (such as repository tech stack updates, security breach disclosures, hiring for specialized AI roles, or new framework deployments) rather than untargeted cold outreach.

By coordinating high-precision, low-volume touches across communication surfaces, growth engines capture high-intent accounts at the exact moment of need.

## Strategic Objectives
- Identify high-intent accounts exhibiting immediate technical buying signals.
- Execute coordinated multi-touch sequences across email and professional platforms.
- Maintain maximum personalization while keeping outbound volume disciplined and spam-free.

## Execution Framework

### 1. Signal Detection & Ingestion
- Monitor public intent signals: new job listings mentioning AI Agent Security, new MCP server implementations, or public discussions around API key management bottlenecks.
- Enrich account profiles and map primary engineering stakeholders (CTOs, AI Architects, Lead Security Engineers).

### 2. Multi-Touch Cadence
- **Touch 1 (Network Context):** Connect on professional channels with a contextual reference to their current architectural stack or published open-source work.
- **Touch 2 (Technical Engagement):** Provide a substantive technical observation or benchmark on recent community discussions.
- **Touch 3 (Direct Outreach):** Deliver a concise, 3-sentence technical briefing highlighting an open-source solution to their immediate stack requirement.

### 3. Quality & Signal Discipline
- **Strict Anti-Spam Guard:** Keep outreach focused, personalized, and bounded to high-relevance accounts (e.g. 5–10 highly researched targets per cohort).
