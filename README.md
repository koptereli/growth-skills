# Agentic Growth Skills & Methodologies

An open-source repository of empirical growth engineering skills, autonomous GTM playbooks, and algorithmic distribution patterns designed for AI agents and growth engineers.

---

## 🎯 Overview

As AI agents become active participants in software distribution, sales development, and technical community building, traditional manual marketing funnels are being replaced by automated, programmatic, and citation-driven growth loops.

This repository codifies verified, field-tested methodologies across major algorithmic attention surfaces (Search, LLM Answer Engines / GEO, GitHub, LinkedIn, Technical Communities, and Developer Package Registries) into structured, machine-executable skills.

## 🔬 Research & Empirical Sourcing

The methodologies in this repository are synthesized from leading empirical research labs, live platform distribution experiments, and developer ecosystem field reports, including:
- **[Enso Agentic Growth Lab](https://www.enso.bot/research)** research datasets and platform experiment ledgers (`EXP-001` through `EXP-009`).
- **Open-source agent ecosystems** (OpenClaw, Model Context Protocol / MCP, LangChain, Hermes).
- **Independent Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) & SERP studies**.
- **Community-contributed distribution experiments**.

New sources and experimental records are reviewed, tested, and incorporated through continuous weekly research cycles.
